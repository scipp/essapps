# Contributing to ESSapps

ESSapps is an early prototype of applications for data reduction at ESS.
Its design and interfaces change often, so a change from outside the development team would likely be out of date before we could review it.
We do not accept pull requests from outside the team at this stage.

This includes pull requests written by AI agents.
We use AI ourselves, and would rather run our own agents than review the work of others.
